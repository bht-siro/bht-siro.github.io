Meet SIRo at Berlin Science Week

6–8 November 2026 · HKW, Berlin

Plan your visit ↓

This event has ended. The announcement and visitor information below are retained for reference.

Join us at the Robo4Care stand to explore how robotics could support care professionals. Try robot teleoperation in a simulated medication-handling task and talk to us about soft robotic hands, human control and the challenges of assistance in care.

Try teleoperation for yourself

At the IFAF Berlin stand, Robotik: Rückenwind für die Pflege, visitors can use an input station to guide a robot through a medication-handling task in simulation. The wider Robo4Care exhibit also introduces exoskeletons and their potential to support physically demanding care work.

Our research connects compliant robotic hands with teleoperation interfaces. Medication preparation provides a setting for investigating fine-motor manipulation, operator assistance and the limits of robotic support.

Part of Robo4Care

Robo4Care — Robotics for Care Enhancement — brings together BHT, ASH Berlin and HTW Berlin with research and practice partners. Funded by the State of Berlin through IFAF Berlin, the project runs from January 2026 to December 2027.

At SIRo Lab, we develop manipulation and teleoperation systems for research in ambulatory care. Explore our work in Robo4Care →
