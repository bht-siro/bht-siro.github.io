PhD Researcher

Ranjit Santhanaraman

How does someone with no training in operating a humanoid control it with ease, and feel what the robot feels?

A haptic input station built from two cobot arms, for teleoperating humanoid robots.

Ranjit.Santhanaraman@bht-berlin.de · ORCID 0009-0008-0842-4541

Research focus

Ranjit develops a haptic input station for teleoperating humanoid robots. The station is designed to use two Franka Research 3 cobot arms to track the operator’s arm movements and return forces measured at the remote robot’s hands. His published work addresses the spatial arrangement of the input arms. Separate internal demonstrations using a Franka Panda arm have shown single-arm teleoperation and haptic feedback.

His method represents the human arm’s reachable workspace as a continuous mesh, generated using forward kinematics and configuration-dependent joint limits. It compares this workspace with the cobot’s reach to identify candidate base placements. One arm is optimised and the second is mirrored. The arm closeness index and joint closeness index evaluate the resulting configurations.

Within tele.interaction, Ranjit is developing the input station for TIAGo Pro teleoperation. Mounting and functional integration of a SIRo Soft Hand are currently under way, initially on one side of the robot.

Background

He joined the lab in September 2024 after completing a master’s in robotics at RWTH Aachen. His doctoral research is supervised by Hannes Höppner.

His student team received RWTH International Academy’s Student Project Grant 2022 for a project estimating soiling on solar panels using aerial vehicles.
