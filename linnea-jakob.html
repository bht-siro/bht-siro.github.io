PhD Researcher

Linnéa Jakob

What becomes possible when elasticity spans more than one joint?

3D-printed variable stiffness actuators and elastic couplings across multiple joints.

Linnea.Jakob@bht-berlin.de · ORCID 0009-0002-9483-4091

Research focus

Linnéa develops variable stiffness actuators based on 3D-printed nonlinear torsional springs. Their geometry determines the spring characteristic and can be adapted through mechanical design.

She also investigates multiarticular coupling, in which elastic elements span more than one joint. Inspired by biarticular muscles, these couplings transfer energy between joints and influence how an arm responds to external forces.

Her work includes mechanical modelling, finite element analysis, CAD design and the development of an arm prototype. The aim is to investigate how coupled elasticities affect dynamic manipulation, including throwing, catching and contact with the environment.

Her doctoral research is conducted in cooperation with TU Wien, supervised by Hannes Höppner at BHT and Christian Ott at TU Wien.
