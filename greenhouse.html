Greenhouse

Compliant manipulation for delicate plants, where excessive grip force damages the crop and careful handling determines the result.

Why the greenhouse

Horticulture is full of soft, irregular, easily bruised objects: ripe tomatoes, peppers, leaves, and stems. These are precisely the conditions compliant hands are designed for. A variable-stiffness hand adapts passively to shape and yields on contact, so the operator can concentrate on the task rather than regulating grip force manually.

The greenhouse is the primary research scenario of our DFG tele.interaction project. The target setup is a TIAGo Pro carrying SIRo Soft Hands, teleoperated through the Visuo-Haptic Input Station, so that we can study how spatial configuration, workspace optimisation and compliant hardware change what a remote operator can do among the plants. That robot has not been in the greenhouse yet. What has been there are student teams with hands they built themselves, and what they found is below.

DFG · Großgeräteinitiative HAW · 533327659 · 2024–2028 Partner: BHT greenhouse laboratory, Gartenbauliche Phytotechnologie, Department V TIAGo Pro + SIRo Soft Hands

The tasks

Our target tasks are taken from commercial tomato cultivation. They are also the tasks our students tackle in the Catch-Up Challenge, so the teaching and the research share the same problems.

Ausgeizen

De-suckering

Removing lateral shoots from tomato plants to direct growth into the fruit.

Entblatten

Defoliation

Strategic leaf removal to improve light exposure and airflow around the fruit.

Einzelfruchternte

Single-fruit harvest

Picking individual ripe tomatoes without bruising them or the plant.

Rispenernte mit Schere

Cluster harvest

Harvesting whole fruit clusters by cutting the stem with scissors.

The research question

Harvesting a tomato by teleoperation requires the operator to judge ripeness, plan an approach through a cluttered canopy and apply enough force to hold the fruit without crushing it, all through a remote interface. We investigate how much of that load the hardware can carry: when the hand adapts to the fruit passively, the operator can attend to the task rather than to the grip, and the morphology handles the fine control.
