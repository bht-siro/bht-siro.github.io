Surgical Instrument Reprocessing

We investigate how a compliant robotic hand can support the handling of surgical instruments before machine cleaning. The target tasks include separating, opening and placing instruments in trays.

The task

In a sterile processing department (in German: Aufbereitungseinheit für Medizinprodukte, AEMP), used instruments may arrive unsorted and interlocked. Preparing them for machine cleaning involves identifying, checking and arranging them, including opening hinged instruments.

In SIRo-AEMP, we investigate these handling tasks using a robot arm, a SIRo Soft Hand and a camera. First instrument grasps have been demonstrated. Reliable separation, opening and placement remain development objectives.

1

Separating instruments

Interlocked and unsorted instruments create uncertain contact conditions. We investigate how an adaptive hand can grasp and separate individual objects.

2

Visual recognition and localisation

Reflective metal surfaces complicate camera-based perception. Recognising and localising instruments under these conditions is a central technical question.

3

Handling sharp instruments

Sharp tips and cutting edges place additional demands on grasp selection and motion planning. We investigate handling strategies that limit unwanted contact and damage to instruments and the hand.

4

Opening hinged instruments

Opening scissors and other hinged instruments requires controlled relative motion within the grasp. We investigate how the fingers, thumb and movable wrist can support this manipulation.

Validating the hand in an application

SIRo-AEMP connects research on variable stiffness, Bowden-cable actuation and programmable fingertip compliance with the requirements of instrument reprocessing. The project examines which handling tasks the hand can support and which require further development.

The findings will inform the next generation of the SIRo Soft Hand and help assess opportunities for transfer into practice. The project also supports preparations for a possible spin-off from BHT.

Funding and partners

SIRo-AEMP is funded by the State of Berlin under ProValid, administered by IBB Business Team GmbH. The nine-month validation project started in April 2026. Industry partner enamentis GmbH specialises in the automation of sterile goods logistics.
