Adrian Sieler

How much of the control can the hardware itself take over?

In-hand manipulation with soft hands, applied to the reprocessing of surgical instruments.

Adrian.Sieler@bht-berlin.de · ORCID 0009-0005-6494-7433

Research focus

Adrian investigates manipulation with compliant hands in SIRo-AEMP, which evaluates the SIRo Soft Hand for surgical instrument reprocessing. The application requires handling instruments that vary in geometry, may be delicate and can be difficult to distinguish.

His research background is in-hand manipulation: repositioning an object within the hand. Contact and deformation make these movements difficult to model in soft anthropomorphic hands. His approach uses the hand’s deformation state as a control variable to simplify the relationship between actuation and object motion.

He also studies how mechanical compliance supports robust dexterity through self-stabilisation, and how these properties can support learning-based manipulation.
