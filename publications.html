Variable Stiffness Actuation via 3D-Printed Nonlinear Torsional Springs
H. Höppner, A. Kirner, J. Göttlich, L. Jakob, A. Dietrich, C. Ott
IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), vol. 10, no. 5, pp. 4324–4331, 2025
SIRo + DLR + TU Wien
IEEE Xplore
Design, implementation, and validation of a novel antagonistic Variable Stiffness Actuator using 3D-printed progressive nonlinear torsional springs. The approach enables precise stiffness control through mechanical design rather than complex control strategies, demonstrating how additive manufacturing can be leveraged for rapid prototyping of compliant actuators.

VSB: Variable Stiffness Based on Bowden Cables: A Simple Mechanism for Soft Robotic Hands
S. Puhlmann, A. Albu-Schäffer, H. Höppner
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), pp. 5556–5562, 2025
DOI
A novel variable stiffness mechanism using Bowden cables for soft robotic hand applications. The mechanism provides a simple, lightweight approach to achieving controllable compliance in robotic fingers, enabling safer and more adaptive grasping.

Throwing beyond Tracking: Velocity-Aware Via-Point Movement Primitives for Release-State Conditioning
R. Abyaad, H. Höppner
Under review, 2025Under review, no public version available yet.
In dynamic tasks such as throwing, the outcome is determined by the state at the moment of release (position and velocity together) rather than by tracking a full trajectory. This work introduces Velocity-Aware Via-Point Movement Primitives (V2MP), which condition on both position and velocity in an augmented state space, demonstrated on the TIAGo Pro platform.
C
Programming Passive Fingertip Deformation for Improved Grasping and Manipulation
S. Puhlmann, L.-C. Weber, H. Höppner
IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), pp. 9175–9181, Abu Dhabi, 2024
DOI
Improving dexterous grasping and manipulation through controlled passive fingertip deformation. Programming the mechanical properties of soft fingertips significantly improves grasping success rates without additional sensing or control complexity.
