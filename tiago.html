TIAGo Pro

A mobile manipulation platform for learning from demonstration, teleoperation research and planned teaching applications.

Research platform

TIAGo Pro by PAL Robotics has two 7-DoF torque-controlled arms with series-elastic actuation, an omnidirectional base and a lifting torso. We use the platform to study learned movements and teleoperation. Teaching applications are planned.

We are developing functional integration of the SIRo Soft Hand, starting with one arm, alongside our teleoperation interfaces. The intended system will combine compliant manipulation, motion learning and operator guidance on a shared platform.

SIRo Soft Hand integration

in development The hands were previously mounted for photographic documentation. We are now working on mounting and completing functional integration on one side first. Operation of the combined system has not yet been demonstrated.

The integration design replaces TIAGo’s wrist roll and pitch axes with the SIRo Soft Hand and its own compliant wrist. This places the variable-stiffness wrist mechanism in the hand assembly.

In this configuration, the arm contributes five controlled degrees of freedom. The hand is intended to provide variable-stiffness actuation and passive adaptation to objects, allowing us to investigate the contribution of compliant mechanics to grasping.

Planned applications include plant handling and assistance with fine-motor tasks in care. The throwing demonstrations shown below use TIAGo’s standard gripper.

Application contexts

TIAGo connects our research with three application contexts, each at a different stage of development.

tele.interaction · DFG

Greenhouse

We plan to investigate teleoperated harvesting and plant handling in the greenhouse. This scenario brings together manipulation in constrained spaces and contact with delicate, variable objects; TIAGo has not yet been tested there.

Robo4Care · IFAF

Care Robotics

Together with care providers, we assess which everyday fine-motor tasks could benefit from teleoperated assistance. Candidate tasks include handling small objects and preparing medication; suitability remains under investigation.

B.Eng. HROB

Teaching

A planned scenario will investigate TIAGo Pro as Scrum Master or team member in the Ball Point Game. This prospective use is separate from the established student robotics challenges, which involve hands and controllers built by the students.

Teleoperation via VHIS

TIAGo is the target robot for the Visuo-Haptic Input Station (VHIS) developed in tele.interaction. Single-arm teleoperation from a Franka Panda arm has been demonstrated internally. The complete dual-arm setup with visual and haptic feedback is under development.

We investigate how compliant hardware affects remote manipulation. Passive adaptation to an object’s shape may reduce the need for the operator to specify individual finger motions. The intended integration will allow us to evaluate this effect on task performance.

Demonstrations

TIAGo performs a throwing motion as part of our research on learning from demonstration. The related work is under review for IEEE ICRA 2027.
